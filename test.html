Outstanding fine

A fine of £200.00 is currently recorded against your licence and is shown on page 1 of this notice. This fine is not payable as part of this request. If your request is approved, the fine will also be lifted and cancelled at the same time as the points are removed, and you will not need to do anything further. If the request is not approved, the fine remains payable and you will be told separately.

Processing time

Once your payment has been received and the reference verified, please allow up to 5 working days for the request to be approved and for your case to be formally opened. The five-day period begins on the working day after the funds clear, not on the day the payment is sent.

Removal of points is not immunity

Removing the penalty points does not make you immune to future penalties. This removal clears the points covered by this request, but the ordinary rules continue to apply from that point onwards. If you commit a further endorsable offence after the removal, new points will be added to your record in the normal way and will count towards any future totting-up threshold exactly as they did before.

In other words, this removal resets your position; it does not grant you a permanent exemption. You should continue to observe all road traffic requirements, because any points you receive next will be treated as a fresh matter and will not be offset by the removal made under this request.

Driving test and written authorisation

Depending on how long the points have been held and on your driving history, you may be required to take a driving test again before you resume driving. If this applies to you, you will be told in writing.

Where a test is required, a written authorisation to drive will also be posted to the address shown on your driving licence. Keep this authorisation with you and produce it if asked. Do not resume driving until you have received it.
